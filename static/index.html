System Architecture

How Verifi Evaluates Risk

Orchestrated with LangGraph state machines for deterministic safety and semantic reasoning.

01

Private Vision & OCR

Incoming screenshots from SMS or WhatsApp chats are parsed directly on your CPU/GPU using WebAssembly Tesseract.js. No private photos are ever transmitted across networks.

02

Deterministic Pattern Scan

Rule heuristics instantly flag unverified domains (.xyz, .top), URL shorteners, and credential harvesting, while isolating real security disclaimers to prevent false alarms.

03

Groq AI Context Reasoning

Open-weight gpt-oss-20b evaluates pretexting, blackmail pressure, and impersonation, composing explanations directly in your native mother tongue.

Zero False-Positive Disclaimer Protection

Unlike naive filters that trigger whenever they see the word "OTP", Verifi parses protective phrases like "Never share your OTP with anyone" and confirms legitimate bank debit notifications are safe.
